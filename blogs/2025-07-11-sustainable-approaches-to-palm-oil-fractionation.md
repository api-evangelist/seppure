Palm oil is the most widely produced, consumed, and globally traded vegetable oil. It is extracted from the fruit of Elaeis Guineensis, a crop cultivated primarily in tropical regions, particularly in Southeast Asia, West Africa, and parts of Latin America. Its unique chemical properties, cost-effectiveness, and high yield have made it essential across a wide array of industries, including food, pharmaceutical, cosmetics, and renewable energy.
