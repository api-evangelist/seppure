From pharmaceutical manufacturing to food processing, many industrial processes heavily rely on solvent-based extraction methods to isolate valuable compounds efficiently. Methods such as ethanol solvent extraction, hexane extraction, and supercritical CO₂ extraction are widely used due to their high yield and selectivity. However, these processes often rely on single-use solvents, generating significant volumes of chemical waste.
