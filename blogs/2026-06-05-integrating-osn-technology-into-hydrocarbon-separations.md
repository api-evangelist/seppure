Hydrocarbons are organic compounds that are solely composed of hydrogen and carbon atoms . They are widely used across industries including petrochemical, chemical, plastics, rubber, and more, as essential raw materials, and they are vital to modern life and its quality. About 85-90% of the global hydrocarbon production is utilized for energy applications, including transportation fuels, electrical power generation, and heating.
