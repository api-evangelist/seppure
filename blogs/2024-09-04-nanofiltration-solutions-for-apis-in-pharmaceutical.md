Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) in pharmaceutical manufacturing are the foundation of modern medicine. Together with excipients , they constitute the two essential components of any pharmaceutical product, with APIs serving as the biologically active substances responsible for delivering the intended therapeutic effect. As a result, the quality, purity, and consistency of pharmaceutical APIs have a direct impact on a drug's safety, efficacy, and overall performance.
