The pharmaceutical industry is one of the world’s most resource-intensive sectors, accounting for 5% of the global Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions every year. Each stage of the pharmaceutical supply chain leaves a significant environmental footprint, from sourcing raw materials for Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) to the energy-intensive chemical synthesis of these materials and solvents. Alarmingly, the carbon emissions involved in synthesizing these molecules often go unmeasured.
